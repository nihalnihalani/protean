## Verdict

Build Protean as the single primary entry, with TESTBENCH-FORGE v2 as a conditional second slide off the shared scaffold and SILICON-FORGE v2 as slide-only insurance -- this is what the weighted axes (Researcher .50, Investor/Sponsor .20 each, Audience .10) and the adversarial rounds jointly support. Protean wins the dominant Researcher axis (the held-out continuous-shape generalization verifier is the one genuine white space, distinct from KernelBench-as-static-eval and from every 2026 paper that tests on its training distribution), owns the only universally legible demo (a live ms speedup stopwatch), and has the strongest env-as-product investor story aligned with HUD's own funded thesis. Its two surviving high-residual risks are both Saturday go/no-go GATES, not unknowns: the harness is near-greenfield (verified: no local hud-blank, ml-template is torchtitan) so lift the Modal decorator and verilog scaffold and check KernelGYM's license to recover the buffer; and the reward is hackable so merge AND adversarially probe the four-layer anti-hack guard before the 8AM kick. De-scope the moat to a parameterized SHAPE sampler over ~5 fixed ops (deferring true op-graph/CSP-DAG to a stretch), enforce a pre-GRPO calibration gate (variance + 20-50% band), reframe 18->40 as a directional Kevin/Dr.Kernel analog, and pre-record everything so the demo never depends on conference WiFi. The win is not the magnitude of the delta -- it is shipping a clean, non-gameable verifier with a generalization curve that proves reasoning over memorization, by 1PM Sunday.


## New opportunities surfaced by research

- Held-out CONTINUOUS-shape generalization verifier is the single defensible, literature-confirmed white space: KernelBench roadmap #74 lists shape-sweep as an unshipped to-do, and Dr.Kernel/DRTriton/Kevin all train and test on the SAME distribution. This (not op-graph, which DRTriton has, nor anti-hacking, which Dr.Kernel has) is Protean's real moat and the answer to 'how is this different from KernelBench-in-a-loop.' Make it the literal money slide: train-shape reward vs held-out-shape reward curve.
- Fold daVinci-kernel's profiling-ratio (PR) reward term (published June 2026, DURING the hackathon) into the Protean grader to close the partial-runtime exploit the phase13 spec does not address -- turns a fresh-paper competitive threat into 'we already integrated this weekend's SotA.'
- verilog cocotb_dv grade.py is a COMPLETE, debugged mutation-kill testbench grader (agent writes suite -> golden gate + hidden mutant-kill fraction + hard cap + fail-closed process-group SIGKILL timeout). TESTBENCH-FORGE transliterates this rather than extending coding-template's timeout-less binary runner -- collapses ~5 of 6 claimed-new components into a port, and the verilog track itself is live proof-of-mechanism for the 'portable to hardware' pitch.
- TRITON_CACHE_DIR baked into the Modal image (Red Hat OCI-caching, Jan 2026) eliminates 30-120s JIT cold-start per rollout -- a 3-6x throughput multiplier almost no competing team will use, directly attacking Protean's #1 throughput risk and the strongest non-obvious Modal integration.
- Reframe all three YC pitches from product-revenue to the HUD Series-A thesis the host itself is funded on ('reusable non-gameable environment sold to labs'), and for TESTBENCH specifically pivot from 'verification-as-a-service' to 'non-gameable verifier environments' anchored on the 2026 reward-hacking crisis (RHB 13.9% vs 0.6%, TRACE 63% detection ceiling, STING 77% weak suites) -- mutation-kill is a programmatic verifier with no LLM judge in the reward path, the exact thing labs are desperate for.
